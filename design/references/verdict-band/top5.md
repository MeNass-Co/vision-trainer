# Verdict band — top 5 Mobbin references

Current fragment (`design/captures/current/progress-data.png`): a bordered pill chip "▲ +0.18" plus a plain caption line "Reliable reading" under the hero CSF number. No unified verdict treatment — the delta and the status live as two disconnected typographic scraps.

## 1. WHOOP — Daily Overview share card, "Training State: Restorative" (score 28/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/36d6035e-3538-47b4-b610-e76e0394339a
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/verdict-band/candidate-1.png
- Convergence: 5 — the "micro-label (caps, muted) over bold verdict word" chunk is the same idiom as Oura's "Optimal" under its score and Eight Sleep's "In range" dot-label; 3+ tier-1 apps converge on decoupling the raw number from a separate, named verdict.
- Tier: 5 — WHOOP, explicit design-benchmark app.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 (→10) — real card inset padding and dividers, dual-ring color coding (yellow recovery / blue strain) stays crisp at small size, caps micro-label sits at correct optical weight below the bold verdict word, no gradient mush.
- Adaptability: 4 — the "caps micro-label + bold verdict word" block lifts directly onto lunar cyan; the dual-ring is decorative context we can drop without losing the core pattern.
- Measurability: 4 — fully visible inside the share-sheet card, high-res, values and word legible.
- One-line why: proves the verdict deserves its own named word ("Restorative"), not just a color and a number — ours never names the state at all.

## 2. Eight Sleep — Sleep Fitness Score, "Out of range" (score 27/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/752cd0a8-8a8b-4d09-a116-4e0cab9f18c4
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/verdict-band/candidate-2.png
- Convergence: 4 — the colored-dot-plus-word micro-verdict also shows up in Oura's contributor rows (colored dot bullets on "Pay attention"/"Optimal") and WHOOP's stat-row indicator dots; a shared idiom for "attach a one-word state to a value without a chip."
- Tier: 4 — Eight Sleep, sleep-tech tier-1, same design class as Oura/WHOOP.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 (→10) — the most restrained execution of the five: single small colored dot, one muted word, thin dashed gauge, no background fill at all. Exactly the "fill restraint" the brief asks for — legible without shouting.
- Adaptability: 5 — the single best fit for our constraints: standalone dot+word under a hero number, no dependency on tab count, imagery, or a 5-cell layout. A near 1:1 swap for our current "Reliable reading" line.
- Measurability: 4 — full screen visible, high-res; the dot's exact hue needs slight zoom but the pattern itself is unambiguous.
- One-line why: shows restraint can still read as a verdict — a dot + one word beats our current unstyled caption line.

## 3. WHOOP — Recovery tab, "Solid Recovery" (score 27/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/7bea0326-7a57-4132-9667-0373012375f9
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/verdict-band/candidate-3.png
- Convergence: 5 — score + colored ring + bold status headline + one explanatory sentence is the canonical WHOOP-class pattern, echoed by Oura's "Up for something fun?" headline+copy and Eight Sleep's score+status+copy stack.
- Tier: 5 — WHOOP, explicit design-benchmark app.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 (→8) — ring stroke is crisp with a rounded cap, color (yellow = moderate recovery) reads instantly, type hierarchy (caps label → giant number → bold headline → muted body) is tight and consistent to the pt.
- Adaptability: 4 — the headline+copy verdict block ports cleanly to our card; the full ring is a bigger structural ask than our current progress screen carries elsewhere (element 16 owns ring language), so this piece specifically should be borrowed for type/copy, not the ring.
- Measurability: 5 — full screen, high-res, every value and word legible, both filled and unfilled ring segments visible (state visible).
- One-line why: pairs the verdict word with one plain-language sentence of "why" — ours states a fact ("Reliable reading") but never a verdict or its reasoning.

## 4. Believe — token detail sheet, "+4.68%" delta chip (score 23/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/a2f59198-7b7f-41b0-a794-becc8233d959
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/verdict-band/candidate-4.png

- Measurability: 4 — small element but fully visible, high-res, easy to extract exact chip padding/radius.
- One-line why: our chip is an outline pretending to be a fill — this shows what a *real* filled delta chip commits to.

## 5. Oura — Home, Readiness "85 · Optimal" (score 22/30)

- Convergence: 5 — the "word-under-score" verdict pattern is shared with WHOOP and Eight Sleep; a clear 3-app convergence.

- One-line why: confirms the word itself ("Optimal") is what does the work — useful corroboration, but the photo-card chrome around it doesn't survive our constraints.
