# Secondary / quiet button — top 5 references

> Current state: "Maybe later" (paywall) and "Done" (calibration) — grey glass pills with a mushy border, stacked directly below a filled primary CTA. Target: quiet but intentional — reads subordinate, never disabled.

## 1. Waking Up — "Day 1 Complete" (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/eacf3e51-fcb5-457a-a168-558be8588f56
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/secondary-button/candidate-2.png
- Convergence: 4 — outline/ghost-secondary-below-filled-primary pattern also seen in Spotify, Philips Hue, Endel.
- Tier: 4 — Sam Harris's meditation app, premium/design-conscious, Calm/Headspace-adjacent.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 — 1px hairline stroke, pill radius exactly matches the primary's, bold type weight identical to primary (hierarchy carried by fill alone, not by weakening the type), fully transparent fill reveals the bg cleanly.
- Adaptability: 5 — mint-on-near-navy is the closest hue analog to our cyan-on-near-black of any candidate; direct remap.
- Measurability: 5 — full native res, both buttons complete and unobscured.
- One-line why: proves you can make a secondary button read "quiet" purely through an outline + shared type weight, with zero opacity/fill compromise that would make it look disabled.

## 2. Obsidian — "Set up sync" onboarding step (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/c464d280-72a1-4984-b7b9-02bed72b99db
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/secondary-button/candidate-3.png
- Convergence: 4 — filled-quiet-secondary-pill pattern, same family as Hatch Sleep and Xbox below.
- Tier: 4 — cult-status, design-forward productivity tool.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 — secondary pill fill is a distinct mid-grey "card" surface (clearly one step up from pure-black bg), centered medium-weight type, height identical to the primary pill, no border needed because fill contrast alone reads as tappable.
- Adaptability: 5 — pure black bg + saturated-accent primary + neutral-grey secondary is exactly our structure; near-zero remap cost.
- Measurability: 5 — full native res, crisp, both buttons fully visible.
- One-line why: the cleanest "just a lighter grey, no border, no drama" execution — proves restraint alone is enough to read as an intentional secondary action.

## 3. Xbox — "Have a new console?" prompt (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/9a84fc24-71e8-4c5b-ba56-9d7b49432913
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/secondary-button/candidate-5.png
- Convergence: 4 — same filled-quiet-pill family as Obsidian/Hatch Sleep.
- Tier: 4 — major first-party Microsoft app.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 — grey fill sits ~15–18% lighter than the near-black bg; both buttons share the same all-caps type treatment, differing only in fill saturation and hue — a deliberate system, not an afterthought; pill height matches the primary exactly.
- Adaptability: 5 — black bg + saturated primary + neutral-grey secondary translates directly to our lunar-cyan-on-near-black structure.
- Measurability: 5 — full native res, both states legible.
- One-line why: shows that keeping type treatment (case, tracking, weight) IDENTICAL between primary and secondary — and varying only fill — is what keeps the secondary from reading disabled.

## 4. Hatch Sleep — "We found your Hatch (Recovery)" (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/5080b0b7-294b-42fe-87f8-e51b24ae42a9
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/secondary-button/candidate-1.png
- Convergence: 4 — same filled-quiet-pill family as Obsidian/Xbox.
- Tier: 3 — respected DTC sleep-tech brand, solid but not benchmark-class.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 — secondary fill is barely lighter than the navy bg (~10%), bold white type keeps it confident rather than washed out; pill height and radius match the primary pill exactly.
- Adaptability: 5 — navy-on-cyan is structurally almost identical to our near-black-on-cyan; trivial hue remap.
- Measurability: 5 — full native res, both buttons complete.
- One-line why: the near-identical hue relationship (cyan primary on dark-blue ground) makes this the most literal template for our own paywall pairing.

## 5. Spotify — Log in screen, password field (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/e7f71f4a-46bf-4491-ad4b-f314153eca57
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/secondary-button/candidate-4.png
- Convergence: 3 — outline-ghost family shared with Waking Up/Philips Hue, though the all-caps micro-type pairing is more unique to Spotify.
- Tier: 5 — undisputed design-benchmark app.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 — crisp single-px hairline, generous horizontal padding, hierarchy carried by TYPE SCALE (smaller, all-caps, tracked out) rather than by weakening fill/opacity — a legitimate alternate technique to the fill-based approach above.
- Adaptability: 4 — pure black bg translates directly; primary here is white-fill rather than our cyan-gradient, so the pairing needs a bit more remap thought than the others.
- Measurability: 5 — full native res, fully visible, crisp.
- One-line why: demonstrates hierarchy-through-scale-and-type-treatment as a legitimate alternative to hierarchy-through-fill — worth considering if a filled-grey secondary ever reads too heavy next to our cyan gradient primary.
