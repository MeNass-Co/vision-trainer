# Tab bar — top 5 references

## 1. Breathwrk — Home screen (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/e9d6739c-d30c-4e9a-bdfe-02c73b8f2ed5
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/tab-bar/candidate-1.png
- Convergence: 5 — pill-fill active-state on a floating glass bar is the dominant pattern; same family seen in Orbit, Moonly, Tiimo, TIDE.
- Tier: 3 — solid, App-Store-featured wellness app; not quite Apple-benchmark tier.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 (8/10) — real blur is unmistakable (the bright hero glow behind the bar visibly bleeds/softens through the material); active "Home" gets a frosted grey pill + bold label weight; icons are optically balanced circle/magnifier/bars; thin hairline traces the whole capsule. Only miss: active state is fill+weight, no hue shift.
- Adaptability: 5 — exactly 3 tabs (Home/Discover/You = our Today/Progress/Settings count), pure monochrome icons remap trivially to lunar cyan, no structural extras (no center button, no badges) to omit.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, uncropped, both active and inactive states clearly legible in one frame.
- One-line why: the cleanest 1:1 structural match to our bar (same tab count, same "icon+label always visible" layout) with the most legible real-blur evidence of the set.

## 2. Orbit — Subscriptions screen (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/ccee144c-65ce-4813-8876-2b4f67017a63
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/tab-bar/candidate-2.png
- Convergence: 5 — same pill-fill active family as Breathwrk/Moonly/Tiimo.
- Tier: 2 — niche subscription-tracker app, not a design-benchmark name.
- Invisibles ×2: 4 (8/10) — starfield bleeds subtly through the translucent bar (confirms real blur, not flat fill); a crisp light hairline outlines the entire capsule; active "Subscriptions" gets a dark-grey pill + bold label; icons (moon phase, calendar grid, gear) are detailed and optically sized.
- Adaptability: 5 — 3 tabs exact match, and the background is a near-black starfield with soft purple bloom that is essentially our own current-capture aesthetic already — the lowest-friction hue remap in the set.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, both states visible, uncropped.
- One-line why: proves the exact "glass pill floating over our own kind of starfield" composition already works at production quality.

## 3. TIDE — Home screen (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/ed413bc9-2772-4650-b2fd-81a3e89bd964
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/tab-bar/candidate-3.png
- Convergence: 4 — active-state-as-filled-chip is the same family as the pill pattern, executed as a rounded-square icon chip instead of a full pill.
- Tier: 4 — respected sleep/wellness app, close analog to Calm-class execution.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 (10/10) — best-in-class real blur of the whole set: the photographic background is demonstrably softened and tinted through the glass, a translucent hairline rings the full capsule, and the active icon sits in its own frosted white chip (fill+weight+implicit tint shift together).
- Adaptability: 3 — 4 tabs (Home/Sleep/Meditation/Sound), one more than our 3, so the pattern needs a tab trimmed; otherwise dark-friendly and hue-neutral.
- Measurability: 4 — full-res, both states visible, though the busy photo background makes exact alpha values slightly harder to read off.
- One-line why: the single sharpest demonstration of "real blur material with tint discipline" — the axis our current muddy-grey bar fails hardest.

## 4. Moonly — Healing screen (score 24/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/d856bd90-3fb0-4b6e-a84f-d3d0485e5a4d
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/tab-bar/candidate-4.png
- Convergence: 5 — same pill-fill active family as Breathwrk/Orbit/Tiimo.
- Tier: 2 — niche astrology/spiritual app, template-adjacent in places.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 (10/10) — the most complete active-state execution in the set: grey pill fill + bold label weight + orange icon/text color shift together, exactly the "fill+weight+color, not color alone" standard the rubric asks for. Photo background visibly blurs through the translucent bar.
- Adaptability: 2 — 5 tabs, two more than ours; the pattern would need real structural trimming, not just a hue swap.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, active and inactive states both legible, uncropped.
- One-line why: the reference for how to make an active state impossible to miss — combine fill, weight, AND a color shift, not just one of the three.

## 5. Tiimo — Focus screen (score 24/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/db1e4b81-fe3b-4b8a-9090-d4085ff07a7d
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/tab-bar/candidate-5.png
- Convergence: 4 — same pill-fill active family.
- Tier: 5 — Tiimo is an Apple Design Award winner; genuine design-benchmark tier.
- Invisibles ×2: 3 (6/10) — clean pill fill + bold weight + animated ring icon on the active tab, optically well-sized icons throughout, but the bar sits on a flat black frame with nothing behind it to blur, so real blur material can't actually be confirmed from this screenshot — the one clear weakness among the finalists.
- Adaptability: 4 — 4 tabs (one over ours) but keeps labels visible on every tab (matches our always-labeled layout more closely than icon-only alternatives), dark-native throughout.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, both states visible, uncropped.
- One-line why: proof that an Apple Design Award-tier bar still leans on the same pill-fill+weight active language — the pattern is validated at the very top of the tier scale, even without visible blur to confirm the material.
