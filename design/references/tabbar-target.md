Why: the Apple TV bar is the closest transferable reference for Vision Trainer's shared navigation: dark iOS material, floating pill geometry, restrained active state, clear labels, and no decorative health-app noise. The reference defines structure, material, and proportion only. Vision Trainer keeps Instrument Cyan and the lunar instrument identity.

Reference boundary: Apple TV uses a five-item media dock plus a separate search circle. Vision Trainer has three product tabs. The transferable target is therefore the low dark material, contained active treatment, label/icon proportions, and safe-area handling, not the exact item count or Apple TV content.
