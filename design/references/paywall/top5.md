# Paywall — top 5 references (Phase 1)

## 1. Mimo — "Max: Your developer journey starts here" (score 28/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/4079e1ee-2739-482a-9c07-cca8835d123b
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/paywall/candidate-2.png
- Convergence: 5 — checkmark feature list + "Most Popular" badge chip + dual plan-card selection + CTA + legal caption below CTA is the near-exact skeleton of our element; same shape shared with Bevel, Cosmos, FotMob.
- Tier: 3 — solid mid-tier coding-education app, not a system-level benchmark, but disciplined execution.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 — badge pill precisely kerned and color-matched to accent; selected plan card gets a real border-only state change (not just a fill swap); radio fill/check is crisp; type ramp (headline bold / feature grey) is consistent to the pt.
- Adaptability: 5 — dark navy → near-black + cyan remap is trivial; 2-card layout matches our plan-card count; caption "Cancel anytime on the App Store" is a literal match for our legal-caption element.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, both selected/unselected plan states visible, feature rows fully legible.
- One-line why: the cleanest 1:1 skeleton match for badge→headline→features→plan-card(w/badge)→CTA→caption, with real (not decorative) selected-state differentiation.

## 2. FotMob — "FOTMOB+" plan list (score 27/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/a0dcc6e5-5bc0-4acc-ba60-0176d677ee22
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/paywall/candidate-4.png
- Convergence: 5 — "Popular" badge chip on a plan card + radio selected state + price/trial subtext rows converges with Mimo, Bevel, Cosmos, and HBO Max's badge-on-card pattern.
- Tier: 3 — high-traffic sports app, mid-tier design maturity but very disciplined here.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 — best-in-class execution of plan-card rhythm: identical corner radius and 1px hairline across all four cards, perfectly even vertical spacing, yellow selected-radio fill exactly on-brand, no visual noise.
- Adaptability: 4 — four stacked cards vs. our single plan card is a bigger count to reference, but the per-card anatomy (badge + title + subtext lines + radio) extracts cleanly to a 1-card version.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, selected vs. unselected radio states both visible across multiple cards in one shot.
- One-line why: the reference for plan-card *rhythm* — spacing, radius, and hairline consistency our current card is missing entirely.

## 3. HBO Max — "Choose Your Plan" (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/1e08937c-4a39-41d5-bdcb-823b9ad0d9d1
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/paywall/candidate-5.png
- Convergence: 4 — plan card with title + big price + bullet feature list + badge on a segmented toggle; shares the plan-card-with-bullets pattern with all other finalists, though its bullets are plain squares rather than icon rows.
- Tier: 4 — major streaming brand, tier-1 recognizable, one of the more benchmark-adjacent apps in this set.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 — crisp 2px white border marking the selected card, exact price hierarchy (large number + small "/month" baseline-aligned), consistent bullet indent and generous card padding.
- Adaptability: 3 — full-bleed segmented-toggle + stacked plan cards is a bigger structural change from our compact single-card layout; more rework to adapt than the others.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, both plan cards and every bullet fully legible.
- One-line why: the cleanest price-hierarchy typography (big number, small unit) and a real selected-border state, worth stealing even if the toggle structure isn't.

## 4. Bevel — "Upgrade to Bevel Pro" (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/29eb56e2-5a4e-41bb-bedd-d8a413c4c012
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/paywall/candidate-1.png

## 5. Cosmos — "Premium" (score 24/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/399667d5-a8d4-42d4-bc4c-ca00e9e4fc60
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/paywall/candidate-3.png
