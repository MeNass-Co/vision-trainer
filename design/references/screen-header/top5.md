# Screen header system — top 5 references

Element: small context chip/label top-left + large hero title + caption below, on a dark immersive screen (not a standard nav bar). Current state: "Today"/"Progress" grey lozenge chip with no job + unanchored hero type ("Set your baseline", "Your sky is dark").

## 1. Flighty — "Eyes on the road" feature announcement (score 26/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/84ddac73-22e9-4202-a933-4aede12cd020
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/screen-header/candidate-1.png
- Convergence: 4 — chip-row → title → caption ramp independently converges with pliability, HBO Max's eyebrow-label pattern, and Apple Fitness+'s label→title→caption→CTA card. Multiple unrelated categories (travel, fitness, fitness-social, streaming) land on the same skeleton.
- Tier: 4 — Flighty is a repeated App Store Editor's Choice, widely cited as a premium-craft benchmark for travel/utility apps.
- Invisibles ×2: 8 — two distinct chip treatments side by side: "iOS 26" as a 1px hairline-bordered outline pill, "PRO" as a solid purple-tinted fill pill with crisp corner radius. Real typographic weight jump from chip (bold caps, tight tracking) to title (heavier weight, larger size) to caption (regular weight, muted grey). Spacing between chip row → title → caption is consistent and intentional, not just "next line."
- Adaptability: 5 — both chip fills are neutral (dark/hairline, purple-tint) and remap trivially to the cyan ladder; survives a single-chip count (we only need one); no search/avatar dependency; dark-only already.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, uncropped, both chip states (outline vs. solid fill) visible simultaneously for direct pixel comparison.
- One-line why: shows two working chip *fill families* (outline vs. solid) in the same shot, which is exactly the ambiguity our current "grey lozenge with no job" needs resolved.

## 2. pliability — "HYROX Race-Ready Hips" challenge unlock (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/4f1c84fd-bb5a-481c-a87a-b3a01756a358
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/screen-header/candidate-4.png
- Convergence: 4 — same chip→title→caption skeleton as Flighty/HBO Max/Apple Fitness+, plus a *second* chip below the title (icon + label), showing the pattern repeats within one screen.
- Tier: 3 — solid mid-tier fitness/social app, well-crafted but not a top-shelf design benchmark.
- Invisibles ×2: 8 — "24 DAYS LEFT" is a genuine filled pill (olive-tinted glass fill, white/yellow text, real padding) sitting directly on a full-bleed dark hero photo — the closest visual analog to our chip floating over the starfield/orb art. "Team pliability" below the title is a second pill with an icon avatar + hairline edge. Both prove the fill needs enough contrast/tint to read against a busy photographic background, unlike our current flat grey lozenge.
- Adaptability: 5 — dark tinted-fill chip remaps cleanly to cyan; structure survives single-chip use; no unavailable UI elements required.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, uncropped, both chip instances plus the title/caption/stats row all visible.
- One-line why: the only reference that puts a filled chip directly on top of an immersive photographic/gradient background the way ours sits on the celestial art — proves the tint/opacity needed for legibility there.

## 3. HBO Max — "The Last of Us" home hero (score 25/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/abe48b50-d14c-43fe-952f-3f367575f7ea
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/screen-header/candidate-5.png
- Convergence: 5 — the eyebrow-label → giant title → small metadata chip → caption pattern is the industry-standard streaming hero convergence (also seen independently in this scout's Netflix, Disney+, and Google TV pulls).
- Tier: 4 — HBO Max is a top-tier, widely benchmarked streaming app.
- Invisibles ×2: 8 — "16" content-rating badge is a crisp small filled circular chip; laurel-wreath "EMMY WINNER" badge is finely kerned; massive title has confident optical size relative to caption; caption is centered with a controlled, readable line length.
- Adaptability: 3 — our app has no ratings/streaming metadata to hang a circular badge chip off of, and the centered-under-hero-image composition differs from our left-aligned top chip, so less of this transfers directly.
- Measurability: 5 — full-res, uncropped, all elements legible.
- One-line why: demonstrates the title-block spacing ramp at its most extreme scale contrast (eyebrow tiny, title huge, caption small) — useful ceiling reference even if the chip itself isn't a 1:1 match.
