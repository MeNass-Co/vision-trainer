# Settings group — top 5 references

## 1. Apple TV — Account settings sheet (score 27/30)
- Mobbin URL: https://mobbin.com/screens/4d1aa83a-5ffa-4086-a734-bc1303fa8cb1
- Screenshot: saved as design/references/settings-group/candidate-1.png
- Convergence: 4 — grouped rounded-card sections with caps-ish section labels ("Sources", "Auto-Play") shared with Google TV, Calm Sleep, DeepSeek in this set; avatar-header-row-in-card pattern also echoed by yope/BeReal (seen but not shortlisted).
- Tier: 5 — Apple system surface (tvOS/iOS settings sheet), the rubric's top tier.
- Invisibles ×2: 5 — real on-state toggle (green fill, crisp white thumb, correct track/thumb ratio), consistent card corner radius across every section, thin hairline dividers *inside* a card separating stacked rows (not between cards), muted caption text clearly de-emphasized under the toggle pair, avatar-initials chip with correct optical sizing next to name+chevron.
- Adaptability: 4 — greyscale card system remaps to lunar cyan trivially (only the toggle's green fill needs the accent swap); avatar row is optional for us; survives our row count; dark-only already.
- Measurability: 4 — high-res, on-state toggle fully legible, chevron weight and row padding measurable; off-state toggle not shown in this crop.
- One-line why: the cleanest "real card system" — every anatomy piece (avatar/nav row, plain chevron row, value+chevron row, toggle pair with shared caption) sits in the same card language with zero drift.

- Screenshot: saved as design/references/settings-group/candidate-2.png

- Tier: 3 — solid mid-tier product, not a design-benchmark app.

- Measurability: 5 — high-res, every anatomy type (toggle on-state, chevron, subtitle, icon) legible and unobstructed.
- One-line why: shows toggle-row + chevron-row + subtitle-row all coexisting in one card exactly the way our Section/Row components need to.
